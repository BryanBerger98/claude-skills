# Creativity toolkit

The techniques an ultrabrain session draws on, and — more importantly — *when* to reach for each. The facilitator picks and sequences these; the user does not need to know their names.

## The backbone: divergence → convergence

Every session is two modes that must never blur:

- **Divergence** (`diverge` phase): generate wide, suspend all judgment, chase quantity and range. Wild and "bad" ideas are fuel — they open adjacent good ones.
- **Convergence** (`converge` phase): cluster, evaluate, prioritize. Judgment is welcome and required here.

The single most common failure is judging during divergence. Announce the mode at every transition and police it: "Park that — we judge next phase."

Use the other three techniques *inside divergence* to break fixation when the obvious ideas run dry. Rotate; never lean on one.

## Inversion

Flip the problem to expose hidden assumptions.

- Ask: **"How would we *guarantee* this fails / make it as bad as possible?"** List the failure modes, then negate each into an idea.
- Or invert a constraint: "What if the thing we assume is fixed were free to change?"
- Best when: the group is stuck on incremental variations of one safe idea.

## Analogies

Borrow structure from an unrelated domain.

- Ask: **"Who else solves a structurally similar problem?"** — games, nature/biology, logistics, hospitality, physical retail, emergency response.
- Map their mechanism back onto the challenge (e.g. "a tutorial level you can't fail" → onboarding that can't dead-end).
- Best when: the problem feels over-familiar and ideas are all from the same mental model.

## SCAMPER

Systematic transformation of an existing idea or product. Push the current best candidate through each lever:

- **S**ubstitute — swap a component, material, person, or rule.
- **C**ombine — merge two ideas, features, or steps into one.
- **A**dapt — borrow something that works elsewhere.
- **M**odify / magnify / minify — change scale, frequency, or emphasis.
- **P**ut to other uses — repurpose for a different user or context.
- **E**liminate — remove a step, feature, or assumption entirely.
- **R**everse / rearrange — flip order, roles, or dependencies.

Best when: you have a promising seed idea and want its variations, or want to mutate a competitor's approach.

## Six Thinking Hats

Force the group to look from one angle at a time instead of arguing across angles.

- **White** — facts and data only: what do we actually know?
- **Red** — gut feeling and emotion, stated without justification.
- **Black** — caution: risks, what could go wrong. (Use in `converge`, not `diverge`.)
- **Yellow** — optimism: best case, why it could work.
- **Green** — new ideas and alternatives. (The divergence engine.)
- **Blue** — process: are we in the right mode, what's next?

In `diverge`, use **green** and **red** hats to *spawn* ideas. Save **black** and **yellow** for `converge`, where they become the risk/upside lens on each top candidate.

## Sequencing heuristic

1. Free association first (cheap momentum).
2. When it stalls → **inversion** (fast, reliably unsticks).
3. Then **analogies** (widens the model).
4. Take the best seed → **SCAMPER** (depth on a candidate).
5. **Six Hats** throughout to switch the lens deliberately.
